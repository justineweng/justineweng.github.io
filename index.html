Hello!

I am a PhD candidate in the Department of Economics at the University of California, Berkeley. My research is in the economics of education, with work on teacher labor markets and access to educational opportunities. My studies are supported by the NSF Graduate Research Fellowship and the NBER PhD Fellowship in Identifying and Developing Mathematical Talent Among Youth.

Prior to graduate school, I worked as a predoctoral research assistant at Stanford and taught high school math in the San Francisco Bay Area. I have a BA in Economics and BS in Mathematics from The University of Texas at Austin.
