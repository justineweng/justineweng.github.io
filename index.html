About

I am a PhD candidate in the Department of Economics at UC Berkeley. Before my PhD, I earned a BA in Economics and a BS in Mathematics (Actuarial Science), both with highest honors, from the University of Texas at Austin.

Prior to graduate school, I worked as a math teacher at Summit Public Schools and San Francisco Unified School District, a predoctoral research assistant at Stanford, and a math content specialist at CK-12 Foundation.

I am a recipient of the NSF Graduate Research Fellowship and the NBER PhD Fellowship in Identifying and Developing Mathematical Talent Among Youth.
